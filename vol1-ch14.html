A daily read of the Chinese tape

Chapter Fourteen · The Other Tape

by Dan Rivers · 2026-10-27
ONE
Narrative
The Day

Overnight, New York voted for the future. In the session that ended before Shanghai opened, the Nasdaq rose 0.82 percent and Nvidia 2.39 — the technology trade bought back with both hands. Micron added nearly three percent, Tesla more than five. Shanghai read the same numbers and walked the other way. The Composite opened at 3,698.44, a few points above Monday's close, touched 3,708.20 in the opening minutes, and then went down in one direction for the rest of the day, without a rally anybody would recognise as one, until the last print landed on the exact floor of its own range: 3,648.30, down 1.33 percent, the low and the close the same number.

The growth boards had tried the other story first. The ChiNext spent its morning more than one percent to the good, and the final hour took all of that and three percent more: it closed at 2,548.15, down 3.41, fifty-four points above the fifty-two-week floor it stamped on Friday. The STAR 50 never had a morning at all. It fell 4.81 percent to 1,252.28 and finished three points above its own fifty-two-week low of 1,249.01 — as near to that line as this index has come. The Shenzhen Component lost 2.50. Across the last twenty sessions the Composite is down 7.64 percent.

Underneath, the day was nearly unanimous. More than 4,900 stocks fell out of the market's roughly 5,500 names, close to nine in every ten, and nearly a hundred finished down more than nine percent. Turnover shrank for a second session, by 147.8 billion yuan to 1.46 trillion. A market leaving in a hurry raises its volume on the way out; a market simply not being met lowers it. Tuesday lowered it.

What rose was Monday's refuge, wearing a bigger armband. All four tiers of the bank column closed higher, from the state giants up 0.82 percent to the joint-stock banks up 1.61 — the only organised green on the board. China Construction Bank and ICBC finished at record highs again, the second session running; Shanghai Rural Commercial Bank added 4.22 for a year high. Shipping and industrial metals kept them company. What fell was everything with a future attached: aviation equipment lost the most with not one of its forty-six names higher; software managed three of a hundred and thirty; semiconductors ten of a hundred and eighty-three, on 176.4 billion yuan of sector turnover — the largest in the market, about one yuan in every eight that changed hands all day.

TWO
Analysis
Why

The obvious question is what New York's rally was supposed to mean, and the tape's answer is: nothing, which is itself the finding. Semiconductors — the sector closest to the American technology trade — took the day's deepest main-force outflow, 7.42 billion yuan net. Components followed at 3.52 and software at 3.34, with batteries, communications equipment and consumer electronics filling out the list behind them. The chain that has carried this tape's best days for a year was funded out item by item, on the morning its American counterparts were bought back.

The money on the other side of that trade is honest about conviction. The joint-stock banks drew 0.66 billion yuan and the state giants 0.35 — together roughly a seventh of what semiconductors lost on their own. That is not rotation. Rotation moves a balance from one column to another; this moved a balance out of the building. The whole-market net figure was unavailable at writing time, and on a day like this the honest reading of what remained is that it did not arrive anywhere in size.

The afternoon explains the totals better than the totals explain the afternoon. The ChiNext was a percent and more to the good with the morning gone; the final hour took that and three percent beyond it. The Composite finished on its floor. Monday's retreat had shrunk turnover by 347 billion yuan; Tuesday's shrank it by another 147.8 on a steeper fall. Volume falling on a down day is not calm. It is absence — fewer hands willing to stand at these prices, and nobody in a hurry to make them stand there.

The smallest corner of the tape kept its own weather regardless. Three robot-related names booked a fourth consecutive limit-up, a motor maker its first of the run; a clutch of food and retail names took a second — twisted dough, dairy, biscuits. While the boards above them broke, the fringe was still trading stories in small sizes. It is the late-drawdown signature: the last money willing to act is the money with the least to lose.

Hong Kong, the other exchange on the same clock, read the day as Shanghai did. As of 15:42 in Hong Kong, with the closing auction still to run, the Hang Seng stood at 22,988.38, down 0.96 percent and near its session low, after touching 23,313.92 in the morning — above Monday's close of 23,211.81. The Hang Seng Tech index was down 1.74 at 3,790.22, nine points above a fifty-two-week floor of 3,781.15. Whatever disagreement was in the air on Tuesday, it was not between the two Chinese exchanges. It was between both of them and the session New York had just finished.

The banks drew roughly a seventh of what semiconductors lost. Rotation moves a balance. Withdrawal removes it.

Historical Parallels
The Record

A close on the day's own floor is one of this tape's oldest prints. What came after, in the file, runs in both directions.

One · 25 April 2022

That Monday the Composite opened at 3,034.27, never held, and closed on the exact floor of its range at 2,928.51, down 5.13 percent — its first close beneath 3,000 since it first stood there in July 2020. More than 4,500 stocks fell and over 760 finished at their floors; the banks and brokers lost least, though they lost. The verdict was not that day. The next session fell another 1.44 percent to a fresh low, and the session after that, 27 April, printed the actual bottom at 2,863.65 before turning to close up 2.49 percent. By Friday 29 April the Composite stood at 3,047.06, back above the level it had broken, five sessions after the close on the floor.

Difference to note: April 2022 was a five-percent break on rising fear — 760 floors in a single day, a two-year level surrendered at speed, turnover swelling to about 900 billion yuan. Tuesday was a one-and-a-third drift on shrinking volume, with money leaving rather than arriving. Same silhouette, different temperature.
Two · 22 January 2024

That session the Composite fell 2.68 percent to 2,756.34, a near three-year low, with 5,184 stocks down — about ninety-seven in every hundred names, and only the second time in the market's history that more than five thousand fell in one day. The bank column was the day's single refuge, and a thin one: the bank index lost 0.21 percent, the shallowest decline on the board, with one joint-stock lender on a ten-session run of gains at a year high. Even so the file had a lower page to show: two weeks later, on 5 February 2024, the Composite printed 2,635.09 in the session, and the reply came the day after, when Central Huijin said it would widen its purchases of exchange-traded funds and the index answered with a 3.23 percent session.

Difference to note: the January 2024 refuge was defensive — banks losing least while everything fell. Tuesday's refuge was the opposite posture: all four tiers closed higher and two of the giants made record highs. A stronger shelter above a shallower break. The file keeps both pages without saying which one Tuesday belongs beside.
The record shows where the road has been, not where it goes next.

Both entries are honest about the two directions the file runs. In April 2022 the floor-close was followed by two more falling sessions and then a five-session repair that carried the index back over the level it had broken. In January 2024 the refuge held its column and the lower low was still two weeks away; the repair arrived only after a state buyer said it would buy. A close on the floor has meant, in this tape's history, an event — never a verdict on what follows it. The record stands for comparison.

April 2022's close on the floor was repaired inside a week. January 2024's was a fortnight short of its own low. The file keeps both pages.
FOUR
Coda
Last Word

While the Composite sat on its own floor, the fringe ate well. A fourth straight ceiling for the robot storytellers, a second for the makers of twisted dough, dairy and biscuits, and one for a motor company nobody had mentioned all month. Same afternoon, same market, two tapes: one finished on the ground, the other was still trading in ceilings and going home for dinner.
