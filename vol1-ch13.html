A daily read of the Chinese tape

Chapter Thirteen · The Refund

by Dan Rivers · 2026-10-26
ONE
Narrative
The Day

The rescue took one session to build and half a session to be returned. On Friday, 4,526 stocks rose on an extra 427 billion yuan of turnover; on Monday morning the ChiNext — Friday's hero — slid until it traded at 2,601.09, more than three percent beneath a close it had fought all Friday afternoon to win. The fifty-two-week floor it touched that day, 2,494.22, was never seriously revisited; the morning low stopped 107 points above it. Nobody needed to test the floor to make the point. The chain that had been rescued was the chain being sold.

The Composite told a calmer story, of the kind only an index can tell. It spent the morning under water, bottoming at 3,664.82, and the afternoon climbing out: the close came at 3,697.52, up 0.19 percent, seven points clear of Friday and less than two beneath the day's high. The market underneath confirmed none of it. Of 5,409 traded stocks, 2,252 rose and 3,157 fell; fifty-nine reached their ceilings, twenty-seven their floors. One session after nearly the whole market rose together, the majority fell together — and the index stayed green, because what rose was heavy.

Monday's casualty list was Friday's leader board. Components — the circuit boards and passive parts — fell 6.79 percent, the worst sector on the board, with four of their sixty-one names higher; the sector's leader rose 2.47 percent while nearly everything beneath it went down. Glass fiber lost 5.40 percent. Electronic chemicals lost 4.33 with not one of thirty-six names up, and precious metals 4.27 with all eleven down. Communications equipment fell 3.67; semiconductors 3.55, with fourteen of one hundred eighty-three names higher.

What rose was what a market buys when it is returning risk instead of taking it: oil-field services up 3.38 percent, with China Oilfield Services up 7.23 and twelve of thirteen names higher; snack foods up 2.93 with twenty-one of twenty-two; pharmacy retail up 2.73 with thirty of thirty-two; the six great state banks up 2.23, all six of them, ICBC up 3.13; games up 2.11. Snacks, savings, medicine and diesel — the shopping list of a market stepping back from the future it bought on Friday.

Friday's rescue was built with an extra ¥427 billion of turnover. Monday's refund gave back ¥347 billion of it. The difference — eighty billion — is what one afternoon of courage cost.
TWO
Analysis
Why

Follow the money and the morning reads like a return counter. On Friday the semiconductor sector drew the largest main-force inflow on the board, about 6.9 billion yuan. On Monday the electronics chain bled roughly 18.4 billion net — components the deepest single wound at 6.5 billion, semiconductors 5.1, communications equipment 4.4. What replaced it arrived in coins: auto parts drew 0.98 billion yuan, medical services 0.77, games 0.71, the big banks 0.37. Friday's buying was one large decision. Monday's selling was one large decision. The money that moved into the vacated space did not arrive with conviction; it arrived to be somewhere else.

Nothing in the overnight tape forced this. The American session that ended before Shanghai opened — Friday's, across the weekend gap — had improved: the Dow lost 1.23 percent and the Nasdaq just 0.21, with the memory names bouncing, Micron up 2.46. Thursday's AI-led rout in New York had spent its force by Friday night, and over the weekend Nomura told clients the worry about AI spending was overdone. Shenzhen answered on Monday with a 6.79 percent decline in components — on a day a memory-module maker reported third-quarter net profit nearly thirty times the year-earlier figure, and its sector fell anyway. When a market sells its best earnings and shrugs at the calmest overnight tape in four sessions, the selling is not imported. It is positioning, going home.

The company window stopped working, too. On Friday, five optical-module companies stood in it with coordinated statements about orders and buybacks, and their chain closed higher. On Monday — two trading days later — one of the five announced a buyback of up to 150 million yuan, and the chain fell anyway, communications equipment among the day's worst six sectors. Statements buy a session. On Monday even a balance sheet could not buy a second one.

The refuge came back the same day the rescue money left. The six banks that were sold on Friday to fund the rescue were bought on Monday, six for six — the same six, at the same prices, in the opposite direction. With WTI holding near 101 dollars a barrel, a five-month zone by Westock's delayed print, the bid for drilling services was arithmetic rather than sentiment. And the shape of the whole day is quieter than its numbers: twenty-seven floors against Friday's nine marks a retreat, not a rout, and turnover shrank by a third of a trillion yuan while fifty-nine names still finished at their ceilings. The crowd did not flee the theatre. It asked for its money back from the front rows and moved to the back.

Hong Kong, the other exchange on the same clock, printed the same division in the same afternoon — as of 15:43 in Hong Kong, with the closing auction still to run, the Hang Seng stood at 23,221.98, up 0.56 percent, carried by Meituan, up 3.56 after a seven-percent intraday swing, and by coal and banks. The Hang Seng Tech index was down 0.27, with SMIC off 2.34 near its fifty-two-week low, while Tencent, which touched 378.00 earlier in the session, worked its way back to a small gain. Same country, same clock, same split: the weights up, the technology heavy.

Friday, 4,526 stocks rose. Monday, 3,157 fell. Across both days the Composite closed within seven points of itself.
THREE
Historical Parallels
The Record

A green index over a falling majority is an old print in this tape. The dates on file point in both directions.

One · 8–9 May 2023

May 2023 was the spring of the state-enterprise revaluation theme, and the eighth was its loudest day. Banks carried the Composite up 1.81 percent to 3,395.00, a ten-month high; Bank of China closed at its ceiling, its first since July 2015. Underneath, the theme's wake ran cold — the ST segment staged a cascade of more than fifty limit-downs, and the ChiNext managed a quarter of a percent. The next session the index rose to 3,418.95 in the morning and gave all of it back and more, closing at 3,357.67, down 1.10 percent, the bank column splitting with more names lower than higher. That morning print was the leg's top. By 25 May the Composite had closed at 3,201.26 — five and a half percent beneath the bank-carried high, thirteen sessions on.

Difference to note: May 2023 was a theme at full cry beside a ten-month high, one day into a mania for state-owned names. Monday's bank bid was the opposite posture — a refuge inside an eleven-week drawdown, six for six higher by 2.23 percent with no ceiling anywhere in the column. Same silhouette; different altitude.
Two · 16 December 2014

The last time the tape divided this cleanly, the index was rising and the majority was not. Eighteen listed brokers hit their ceilings at once that day, and the Composite jumped 2.31 percent to 3,021.52 — while 1,509 stocks fell and 793 rose, nearly two decliners for every gainer. The papers of the day gave retail investors a name for it: fully invested, and missing the move. The divergence then kept running. By mid-January the index stood above 3,300 — a fact the record proves by what it broke on the way down — until 19 January 2015, when regulators punished margin-rule violations at the largest brokerages and the Composite fell 7.70 percent in a single session, to 3,116.35. The ChiNext fell 0.58 percent that day. The crowd that had missed the move was spared the reckoning, and within a season the leadership of the whole market had flipped to the small boards.

Difference to note: December 2014 was the opening chapter of a leveraged bull — new borrowed money chasing financials upward, volume rising into the divergence. Monday was a de-risking day: turnover shrank by 347 billion yuan, and the money moved toward shelter, not toward a theme. Same silhouette; opposite weather.
The record shows where the road has been, not where it goes next.

Both entries are honest about the two directions the file runs. In May 2023 the bank-carried green was the top of a leg, its verdict delivered inside three weeks; in December 2014 the broker-carried green ran another month and ended in a single seven-percent session, with the next leadership already waiting in the boards nobody wanted. An index reading green over a falling majority has meant, in this tape's history, a market rearranging itself — never a verdict on which arrangement wins. The record stands for comparison.

May 2023 topped the next morning. December 2014 ran a month and ended in one session. The file holds both verdicts, and settles neither.
FOUR
Coda
Last Word

The snack shelf had the best day of anyone. Twenty-one of twenty-two companies that make twists, crackers and preserved fruit closed higher — on the afternoon the market handed back its future, it bought snacks. The Ministry of Finance supplied the footnote: stamp duty on securities trades ran 65 percent ahead of last year through September. Churn is the one product this market never stops selling, and the state collects on every trade. On a day like Monday, somebody somewhere is always eating.
