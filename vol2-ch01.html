A daily read of the Chinese tape

Chapter Twenty-One · Zero

by Dan Rivers · 2026-11-05
ONE
Narrative
The Day

The first print of Thursday was 3,646.57 — the low of the day, set in the same tick as its open, forty points beneath Wednesday’s close. In one quotation the market handed back half of the seventy-seven points its afternoon had earned, and the oldest question in this file came with it: was Wednesday an event, or a rumor?

The tape spent the day answering sideways. The Composite climbed out of its opening pit, reached 3,701.59 after lunch, wobbled once in the middle afternoon, and closed at 3,689.91, up 0.10 percent. It finished twelve points beneath its high and three and a half above Wednesday’s close. Every other index on the board did better, from Shenzhen to the small-cap boards in the north; the figures sit below. An index that opens at its low and finishes near its high has barely moved. It has simply traveled.

Breadth did the heavy lifting. Four thousand eight hundred and sixty-nine stocks closed higher against six hundred and twenty-five lower — about eighty-eight names in every hundred, a wider board than Wednesday’s. Eighty-six finished sealed at their ceilings. And on the far edge of the ledger, the count that gives this chapter its name: zero. Not one stock in the market, in a market of nearly five and a half thousand names, closed at its floor. Wednesday had counted ten.

The empty floor is breadth in its purest form. Not one seller in five and a half thousand names had to take the worst price of the day.

  • Main-force money: semiconductors +¥5.66 billion, photovoltaic equipment +¥3.01 billion, batteries +¥2.51 billion; components −¥6.59 billion, communications equipment −¥5.52 billion

The buying was not where Wednesday’s had been. Solar equipment rose 6.23 percent with sixty-five of its sixty-seven names green, and three of them finished at their twenty-percent ceilings. Digital media rose 5.41 with all fourteen of its names up, and advertising and cinema followed — the application layer from Monday, back for a second visit inside a week. Energy metals rose 5.35 with all twelve higher. Precious metals added 5.19 with all eleven, after spot gold broke through 4,100 dollars in the Asian morning.

What fell was Wednesday’s reflex. Communications equipment lost 2.83 percent, with Zhongji Innolight giving back 7.51 of the ten-plus it had earned a day earlier — on 30.78 billion yuan of turnover, the largest single-name volume in the market. Components lost 2.40 percent. Coal lost 4.20 with barely a fifth of its names green, the receipt for a night in which crude fell by nearly a tenth. Brokers slipped 0.20 percent, most of their names lower.

The two links of the hardware chain that had led Wednesday’s recovery were sold for the whole of Thursday, and the market they left behind rose anyway — wider than Wednesday, with an empty floor. A rotation and a retreat can look identical at three o’clock. The count of stocks is what tells them apart, and Thursday’s count had nobody leaving.

TWO
Analysis
Why

Turnover was 2.118 trillion yuan — roughly 80 billion more than Wednesday, and the second consecutive day above two trillion. The leaders were sold, and volume rose anyway. That is the arithmetic of reallocation: the money that came out of the optical chain did not go home. It went across the aisle.

The exits and the entrances confirm it. Components gave up 6.59 billion yuan of main-force money and communications equipment 5.52 billion — the two largest exits on the board. The largest entrance belonged to semiconductors: 5.66 billion yuan in, with the sector up 1.69 percent and a hundred and fifty-six of its hundred and eighty-three names green. Of the three links that had led Wednesday — optics, components, chips — the market kept the one with a file behind it. The memory maker ChangXin more than doubled its buyback range, to as much as five billion yuan, and the industry’s count of global third-quarter sales came in at a record 517.7 billion dollars.

Solar took in 3.01 billion yuan and batteries 2.51, with nearly every battery name green. Advertising took in 1.93. The intake spread across eight or nine boards; the exits sat in two. That shape — narrow selling, broad buying — is what the empty floor was made of.

The exits sat in two boards. The intake spread across eight. An empty floor is built from exactly that shape.

The overnight tape in New York printed the same trade in a different currency. On the projections, election night had returned a Congress inclined to regulate the industry that had carried the year, and money moved accordingly: the Dow up 3.03 percent against a 3.13 percent fall in the Philadelphia semiconductor index — value taken up, compute put down, on one night. Nvidia lost 3.17 percent and Micron seven. Apple added 5.31 and Microsoft 6.68. The ten-year yield eased to 4.894 percent, and the index of Chinese companies listed in New York rose 3.49 — the tape’s own verdict on a night the dollar slipped.

Whether one market caused the other’s trade is a question the tape does not answer. It records only that within fourteen hours, two exchanges arrived at the same decision: sell the thing that had just bounced, keep the thing with a filing behind it.

Hong Kong, on the same clock, ran the same move at a different amplitude. At 15:40 there, with the closing auction still to run, the Hang Seng was up 3.50 percent and Hang Seng Tech up 5.29. The southern exchange holds more of the names that answer a falling dollar: Tencent up 5.53 on the release of its new Hunyuan model — 770 billion parameters, with visual understanding and computer operation built in. Meituan added 8.05 and Alibaba 7.46, and the cloud and software names finished in double digits. What fell there was what fell here: the optical cable maker down nine percent, the equipment names down ten. Same country, same afternoon, two magnitudes.

The domestic file was plumbing, not headlines. A third tender of the swap facility that lets brokers borrow cash against their bonds went to twenty-four of them for more than 40 billion yuan. The loan program that funds listed-company buybacks was widened again, its rate lowered to one percent, with bank applications reported above 200 billion. The eighteen active ETFs that opened the week finished their raise — about 36 billion yuan among them, part of it earmarked for building positions. None of it carried a timestamp near the open, the noon, or the close. It is the floor under the building, not the hand on the switch.

Which leaves the zero, the number the title borrows. Eighty-six ceilings, no floors, a wider board than the day before, on the second straight day of two-trillion turnover. A market can rise on a narrow list or it can rise everywhere at once; Thursday’s answer was everywhere at once. The empty floor is a count, not a call.

THREE
Historical Parallels
The Record
The record shows where the road has been, not where it goes next.
One · 7 July 2020

A Tuesday, the day after the Composite had risen 5.71 percent — the fourth day of that July’s launch. The market opened above 3,400 for the first time in two and a half years, and the brokers, the engine of the run, were sold from the first hour. More than half the sector finished lower, eight of its names by more than five percent, and the Composite closed at 3,345.34, up 0.37. Turnover rose anyway, to 1.74 trillion yuan, a fourth consecutive day above one trillion. Breadth narrowed to 2,141 names up against 1,540 down.

What followed: the rally ran four more sessions and printed the high of the cycle on 13 July, at 3,458.79 in the intraday. Six days after the brokers were sold, the Composite surrendered 4.50 percent in a single session, closing at 3,210.10, with more than two hundred stocks sealed at their floors.

Difference worth keeping: that day-after narrowed the board — two thousand names up against fifteen hundred down — while Thursday’s widened it, and the names 2020 sold had carried the run for weeks while the names Shanghai sold were one trading day old. In the record, the line between a rotation that feeds a tape and one that drains it showed up a week later, not on the day.
In 2020 the top came four sessions after the leaders were sold. The reckoning came three sessions after the top.
Two · 25 September 2024

A Wednesday, the day after the policy launch that had lifted the Composite 4.15 percent. The market opened at 2,901, ran past 2,950 in the morning, and gave most of the gain back before the bell: it closed at 2,896.31, up 1.16 percent. Turnover printed 1.157 trillion yuan — the first trillion-plus session in four and a half months — and the board was broad: 4,164 names up against 1,011 down, sixty-six at their ceilings, five at their floors.

What followed: the policy week kept its schedule. The next afternoon the Politburo met and the Composite closed above 3,000, up 3.61 percent. The month ended with an 8.06 percent session on 2.59 trillion yuan of turnover. After the holiday the market opened more than ten percent higher and closed up 4.59 — and gave back 6.62 the following day.

Difference worth keeping: 2024 ran on a schedule — a consultation draft the first evening, a Politburo meeting the second afternoon — so the follow-through was booked in advance. Thursday’s had plumbing behind it, and Wednesday’s own tape, with no date on either. The September session also faded from its morning high, a mark Thursday never printed: its low was its first price.

The second day is the market’s receipt. In both accounts the reckoning came a week later.

FOUR
Coda
Last Word

Every board keeps two columns at its edges — the ceiling count and the floor count. Wednesday’s read a hundred and twenty-one against ten. Thursday’s read eighty-six against a blank.

An empty floor certifies nothing about a building. It records only that nobody had to leave it in a hurry. On a day that repriced half of Wednesday’s rally in the first tick and quietly took all of it back by three o’clock, the rarest number on the exchange was the one that never printed — not red, not green, just a seat nobody was forced to take.

The lights stayed on in every room. Nobody used the fire exit. That was the day.
