A daily read of the Chinese tape

Chapter Fifteen · One Hundred and Seventy-Seven

by Dan Rivers · 2026-10-28
ONE
Narrative
The Day

On Tuesday, ten of the one hundred and eighty-three semiconductor names listed in Shanghai and Shenzhen closed higher. On Wednesday, one hundred and seventy-seven did. Nothing about the industry changed in the eighteen hours between the two counts. The column changed, and it changed before the opening bell: all four indices gapped up and not one of them ever traded a single point below Tuesday's close.

The Composite spent the session inside a range of thirty-two points, from 3,652.37 to 3,684.49 — a floor four points above where Tuesday finished, a ceiling three points above where Wednesday finished. It closed at 3,681.47, up 0.91 percent. The number puts the day in the small column: a thirty-three point gain is not a session anyone will describe at dinner. What it describes is a market that refused, for five and a half hours, to give back a single point of its opening bid.

The growth boards carried the rest. The ChiNext opened at its own low of 2,562.90, never looked at Tuesday's close again, and finished at 2,634.76, up 3.40 percent and thirteen points short of its high. The STAR 50 added 2.45, the Shenzhen Component 2.02. The gap between the ChiNext and the Composite is the same gap this tape wrote about a fortnight ago, run backwards: then the banks held the index up while the growth boards slid; on Wednesday the growth boards ran and the banks stood still.

Breadth flipped whole. More than 4,100 of the market's roughly 5,500 names closed higher, against 606 that managed it on Tuesday — close to seven times as many, in one session, with no weekend in between. Turnover came back with them: 1.63 trillion yuan, 167.5 billion more than the day before. Tuesday had taken 147.8 billion out of the count while the market fell; Wednesday put 167.5 billion back while it rose. The two numbers are within twenty billion of each other, which is the tidy part. The untidy part is that they describe opposite things.

The losers were almost exactly Tuesday's survivors. Liquor fell 2.20 percent and did it with a kind of completeness — nineteen names, nineteen declines, the best of them down 0.22. The joint-stock banks gave back 0.89 with two of nine still higher, the brokers 0.87 with ten of forty-eight up. Gold miners slipped 0.82 as the metal itself eased in the overnight session. Marine equipment and aquaculture went with them.

TWO
Analysis
Why

The number worth keeping is not the index. It is 19.4 billion yuan — the combined main-force inflow into communications equipment, semiconductors and components in a single session. Tuesday took 7.42 billion out of semiconductors alone. Whatever else happened overnight, the money came back, and came back larger.

It came back out of the battery complex, which is the day's quiet joke. Batteries closed 0.24 percent higher and lost 1.43 billion yuan net — the largest outflow on the board. A sector can rise and still be the till. Behind it the list reads like a funding schedule rather than a verdict: brokers 1.38 billion out, software 1.08. Software even finished a shade higher while being sold. On a day when four thousand names rose, being sold was not a judgement on a business. It was an entry on the payments side.

Two consecutive New York sessions had bid the memory and accelerator complex. On the night of the 26th the Nasdaq added 0.82 percent and Micron 2.98; on the night of the 27th the Nasdaq added 1.18 and Micron another 2.77, with Nvidia up 0.90 and Tesla 4.04. Shanghai's semiconductors fell 4.54 percent through the first of those nights and rose 4.20 through the second. Two near-identical inputs, two opposite answers, twenty-four hours apart. A market reading the news would have done the same thing twice.

The only hard domestic number of the day was the statistics bureau's nine-month industrial profit release: 6.03 trillion yuan, up 15.5 percent, with joint-stock companies up 20.8 against 13.1 for the state-controlled group and 8.7 for private firms. It was published, it was absorbed, and the tape did not organise itself around it. The day's shape had been decided earlier, in the column counts, by whoever was holding cash and waiting for the second New York night to confirm the first.

That leaves the arithmetic of weights, which is the part readers of the Composite keep mislaying. Four thousand names rose and the headline index gained 0.91 percent, because its heaviest members — the banks, the liquor makers, the insurers — either sat the session out or fell in it. The ChiNext, unencumbered by any of them, gained 3.40. Same morning, same buyers, thirty-three points on one index and eighty-seven on the other. The Composite is not a measure of what happened in Shanghai on Wednesday. It is a measure of what happened in Shanghai after the largest companies were allowed to vote.

Hong Kong kept the same hours and reached the same conclusion, harder in technology than in the broad index. As of 15:41 in Hong Kong, with the closing auction still to run, the Hang Seng stood at 23,109.61, up 0.30 percent on Tuesday's close of 23,041.32, and the Hang Seng Tech index at 3,851.62, up 1.35 — a larger gain than Shanghai's growth boards' and taken from a lower base, after its own 1.48 percent fall the previous session. Both exchanges inside this one country, one morning, one direction, and for once that direction was also New York's.

Batteries closed higher and lost 1.43 billion yuan. A column can rise and still be the till.
THREE
Historical Parallels
The Record

A rebound is the easiest print in this tape to misread. The file has many days shaped like Wednesday, and their next pages are not the same page.

One · 3 February 2020

The first session after the Spring Festival opened into a gap and stayed in it. The Composite fell 7.72 percent to 2,746.61, the Shenzhen Component 8.45, the ChiNext 6.85. Of some 3,700 listed names, 155 rose, and 3,177 finished flat on their floors — more than four in five of the entire market. Turnover shrank to 519.5 billion yuan, because a market that gaps down needs nobody's agreement to fall. The reply came the next morning and it was broad: 2,783.29 on the Composite, up 1.34, the Shenzhen Component up 3.17, the ChiNext up 4.84 to 1,882.69 on more than 900 billion yuan of trade. Three further rising sessions followed, to 2,875.96 by the Friday of that week.

Difference to note: February 2020 was a seven percent gap with a single named cause, 3,177 floors beneath it, and policy money already moving that morning — 1.2 trillion yuan of reverse repos and a ten-basis-point cut in the operation's rate. Tuesday's break was 1.33 percent on 606 advancers, with no announcement attached and no floors worth counting.
Two · 7 April 2025

That Monday the tariff shock arrived whole. The Composite closed at 3,096.58, down 7.34; the Shenzhen Component 9.66; the ChiNext 12.50 to 1,807.21, its worst single session on record; the STAR 50 9.22 and the Beijing exchange 17.95. Across the market, 106 names rose and 5,284 fell, and more than half of every stock listed in the country finished on its floor. Turnover swelled to 1.62 trillion yuan, 459.7 billion more than the session before, because this fall was made out of volume rather than absence. Central Huijin said in the closing minutes that it had added to its exchange-traded fund holdings. The next session the Composite rose 1.58 percent to 3,145.55 and the ChiNext 1.83 to 1,840.31 — a seventh of the ground the ChiNext had surrendered the day before.

Difference to note: April 2025's reply was arithmetic and small, a 12.50 percent fall answered by 1.83, and it arrived with a state buyer named before the bell. Wednesday's was the other proportion: 3.40 percent answered a 1.33 percent decline, and no announcement was made by anybody.
The record shows where the road has been, not where it goes next.

What the file establishes is proportion, not direction. Its two deepest modern washouts drew next-day replies of 4.84 and 1.83 percent — one that had repaired the ground inside a week, one that had not until a buyer with a balance sheet said so out loud. Wednesday's reply was larger than either, off a smaller break, in a market where nobody had to be rescued. Whether that makes it the first page of the 2020 pattern or the first page of something else is not a question the record answers on the first page. It never has. The record stands for comparison.

The file keeps both replies and both breaks: 4.84 against 6.85, 1.83 against 12.50.
FOUR
Coda
Last Word

Somewhere on a side street there is a shop with nineteen bottles of liquor on one shelf, and on Wednesday every one of them was marked down. There is no drama in that. It is what a shelf looks like on the day the money goes to look at something else — the same glass, the same year on the label, nobody asking. The shopkeeper does not read the tape. He knows only that the shelf has been full since spring, and that a full shelf is not the same thing as a quiet one.
